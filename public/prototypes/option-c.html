Chapter 1

The hunger crisis
hiding in plain sight.

3.3 million college students in America go hungry every single day. They sit in lecture halls with empty stomachs. They choose between textbooks and meals.

They drop out — not because they can't handle the coursework, but because they can't afford to eat. This is happening at universities across all 50 states, right now, to students you'd never suspect.

Chapter 2

From kitchen to campus
in three steps.

1

Students sign up

Any student at a partner campus registers in under 2 minutes. Pick your meal times and pickup spot. No paperwork, no stigma, no questions asked.

2

Kitchens prepare

Our partner kitchens prepare fresh, nutritious Sattvic meals every morning. Each meal costs just $5 to make — funded entirely by donors like you.

3

Volunteers deliver

Trained student volunteers pick up meals and deliver them across campus. Students scan a QR code, grab their meal, and get back to class.

Chapter 3

Meet Raj.

Raj Kumar was a sophomore engineering student. Coming from a low-income family, he often went entire days without eating. His grades started slipping.

Then a friend told him about World Food Movement. He signed up in two minutes and started receiving free meals at campus. Within a semester, his GPA went from 2.1 to 3.6.

"I was choosing between eating and dropping out. WFM gave me back my future."

Raj Kumar, Engineering Graduate, Class of 2024

He graduated with honors and now works as a software engineer. Stories like Raj's happen every day — because someone like you funded a $5 meal.
