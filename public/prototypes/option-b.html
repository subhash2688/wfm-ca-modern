The crisis

3.3 million students
go hungry every day.

They sit in lecture halls with empty stomachs. They choose between textbooks and meals. They drop out — not because they can't handle the work, but because they can't afford to eat.

How it works

Three simple steps

1

Students sign up

Register in under 2 minutes at any partner campus. Pick your times and location. No paperwork, no stigma.

2

Kitchens prepare

Fresh, nutritious Sattvic meals every morning. Each costs just $5 — funded entirely by generous donors.

3

Volunteers deliver

Student volunteers deliver meals. Scan a QR code, grab your meal, get back to learning.
