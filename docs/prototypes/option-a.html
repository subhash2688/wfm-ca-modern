The crisis

3.3 million students face hunger every single day.

They sit in lecture halls with empty stomachs. They choose between textbooks and meals. They drop out — not because they can't handle the work, but because they can't afford to eat.

How it works

From kitchen to campus

01

Students sign up

Any student at a partner campus registers in under 2 minutes. Pick your meal times and location. No paperwork, no stigma, no questions asked.

02

Kitchens prepare

Our partner kitchens prepare fresh, nutritious Sattvic meals every morning. Each meal costs just $5 — funded entirely by donors like you.

03

Volunteers deliver

Trained student volunteers deliver meals across campus. Students scan a QR code, grab their meal, and get back to learning.
