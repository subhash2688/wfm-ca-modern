The problem
36 %

of college students skip meals

Millions of students are choosing between food and their future.

They sit in lecture halls with empty stomachs. They choose between textbooks and meals. They drop out — not because they can't handle the work, but because they can't afford to eat. This is the hidden hunger crisis in American higher education.

How it works

Simple. Dignified. Effective.

1 Sign up

Students register in 2 minutes

Pick your campus, meal times, and pickup spot. No paperwork. No stigma. No questions asked.

2 Prepare

Kitchens cook fresh meals daily

Nutritious Sattvic meals prepared every morning. Each costs $5 — funded entirely by donors like you.

3 Deliver

Volunteers bring meals to campus

1,200+ student volunteers deliver meals daily. Scan a QR code, grab your meal, back to class.
